## 🌄 About

MOUNTAIN-HONEY is a premium honey sourced from untamed high-altitude regions. Each drop represents pure nature—completely raw, naturally unfiltered, and packed with the essence of wildflower ecosystems that thrive in mountain peaks.

### Why MOUNTAIN-HONEY?

- **100% Pure Polyflora** - A blend of wildflowers from pristine mountain peaks
- **High Altitude Sourced** - Harvested from bees in elevated regions (3000m+)
- **Raw & Unfiltered** - No heat treatment, no processing, no additives
- **Untamed Quality** - Nature's perfect product, untouched by industrial processes
- **Rich Nutritional Profile** - Packed with enzymes, minerals, and antioxidants
